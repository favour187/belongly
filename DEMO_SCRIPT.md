# 🎬 Belongly — 90-Second Demo Script
| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Dashboard hero + stats | "1 in 6 people on Earth feels lonely — the WHO linked it to 871,000 deaths a year in 2025. 281 million of us live outside our birth country, and millions of students change schools every year. The loneliest window is the same everywhere: your first 30 days somewhere new. This is Belongly — nobody should have to start over alone." |
| 0:15–0:40 | Find My Buddy — change city/languages/interests, watch scores move | "I'm Amara, 16, just landed in Toronto. Belongly scores every verified local buddy — same city, shared language, shared loves — and shows me exactly *why*. Watch: I change my interests… and every match re-ranks live. No black box. Sofia, 94% — we both love art and food." |
| 0:40–0:55 | Click Say Hello → modal | "One tap, and Belongly doesn't just say 'chat' — it suggests a safe first meetup: public library, Saturday, a mentor present. Because safety is the product: verified buddies, public places, no private data, ever." |
| 0:55–1:15 | Journey — tick 2–3 quests | "Then the First-30-Days Journey walks us through four weeks of small wins together: find your safe places, master one bus route, teach your buddy a recipe from home. Every quest earns Roots — and the final one is writing advice to the *next* newcomer." |
| 1:15–1:28 | Ask a Local — post or answer; quick Circles scroll | "Stuck? Ask a Local — anyone can answer, and today's newcomer becomes tomorrow's local. And Circles turn one buddy into your people." |
| 1:28–1:30 | Dashboard | "Belongly. Nobody should have to start over alone. Thank you." |

**Tips:** Zoom browser to 110–125%. Practise the click-path once. Screenshots for Devpost: Dashboard · Buddy scores · Journey · Ask a Local · Safety.
