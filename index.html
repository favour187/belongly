💚 My Connections

🛡️ Safety is the product

Connecting minors and newcomers with new people only works with serious guardrails. These are enforced in the design, not hidden in terms & conditions.

✅ Verified buddies only

Buddies are verified through a member school, university, library or community organisation. No verification, no matching.

📍 Public first meetings

First meetups are suggested only in public places (library, school, café, community centre) — never homes, never cars.

🔒 No private data needed

First names + city only. No home address, no phone number, no school timetable is ever required or shown.

🚨 One-tap report & block

Every profile, question and Circle has report/block. Reports from minors are prioritised and reviewed by the verifying organisation.

👀 Trusted-adult visibility

For under-18s, a parent/guardian or school mentor can view buddy matches and first-meetup plans.

🌐 Moderated in your language

Ask-a-Local is moderated per city, with community moderators for the main local languages.

The global need, in one paragraph

The WHO Commission on Social Connection (June 2025) found 1 in 6 people worldwide feels lonely, linked to an estimated 871,000 deaths a year, with the highest rates among teenagers (17–21% of 13–29-year-olds) and in lower-income countries. At the same time 281 million people live outside their country of birth and 117.3 million were forcibly displaced (UNHCR) — before counting the millions of students who simply change school, city or neighbourhood every year. Belongly doesn't try to "cure loneliness" with an app. It does one concrete thing: it makes sure nobody faces their first 30 days somewhere new, alone.
